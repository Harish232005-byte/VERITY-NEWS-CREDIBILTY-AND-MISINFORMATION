1. Project Abstract & Motivation

In modern digital ecosystems, deceptive misinformation, sensationalist clickbait, and emotionally charged propaganda propagate six times faster than verified news. Verity is a multi-dimensional Natural Language Processing web platform designed to evaluate news credibility, quantify sensationalism, extract verifiable claims, and render Explainable AI (XAI) feature attributions.

2. Five-Stage NLP Pipeline Architecture

1 Preprocessing & Tokenization

Regex-based boundary detection, abbreviation isolation, lowercase normalization, stopword filtering.

2 TF-IDF & N-Gram Extraction

Sublinear term frequency scaling, uni-gram and bi-gram feature extraction across a 2,300+ term vocabulary.

3 Stylometric & Readability Profiling

Flesch Reading Ease, Flesch-Kincaid Grade Level, Coleman-Liau, Gunning Fog, Type-Token Ratio, Hapax Legomena.

4 Sentiment, Emotion & Bias Radar

Valence polarity (-1 to +1), subjectivity scoring (0 to 1), and emotion breakdown (fear, anger, trust, surprise).

5 Composite Credibility Synthesis

Multi-factor weighted index combining ML probability (35%), stylometrics (25%), objectivity (15%), domain trust (15%), and fact-checks (10%).

3. Mathematical Formulations

Flesch Reading Ease (FRE):

FRE = 206.835 - 1.015 * (Total Words / Total Sentences) - 84.6 * (Total Syllables / Total Words)

Flesch-Kincaid Grade Level (FKGL):

FKGL = 0.39 * (Total Words / Total Sentences) + 11.8 * (Total Syllables / Total Words) - 15.59

Type-Token Ratio (TTR - Lexical Diversity):

TTR = |V| / N (Unique Word Types / Total Word Tokens)

4. Explainable AI (XAI) Feature Attribution

Rather than presenting a "black box" prediction, Verity evaluates token-level feature coefficients from the calibrated classifier and stylometric lexicons. Each token is categorized dynamically into:

  • Credible Cue: Features strongly correlating with verified institutional journalism (e.g. confirmed, peer-reviewed, percentage, regulatory).
  • Suspicious Cue: Features associated with deceptive patterns (e.g. secretly, whistleblower, shadow syndicate).
  • Sensational Cue: Emotionally loaded terms and clickbait triggers (e.g. miracle, jaw-dropping, catastrophic).
